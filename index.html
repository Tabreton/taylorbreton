About me

Science for healthier coasts.

I'm a PhD student studying seagrass ecology, restoration, and the environmental factors that shape successful coastal restoration.

My work sits at the intersection of field ecology, experimentation, propagation, and applied restoration. I'm particularly interested in understanding how biological variation and environmental conditions influence restoration outcomes.

Featured project
Vallisneria americana

Growing better seagrass for restoration.

My research investigates how genotype, sediment, fertilizer, aeration, and planting conditions influence the propagation and establishment of Vallisneria americana.

The goal is to turn ecological knowledge into practical guidance for restoration practitioners.
